<p>At the end of every year Spotify (the music streaming service I use) sends me a list of my <em>Top Songs of the Year</em> (that is, the songs I've listened to most throughout the year). My list is always heavily dependent on the books I've written that year, due to my great love of book soundtracks. So it was no surprise that all the songs I had on my completely unofficial soundtrack for <em>Everything We Need</em>, Book Four of my <em>Finding Forever</em> series, topped my <em>Top Songs</em> list for 2017. Although they did have some competition this year from a Norwegian pop song (<a href="https://youtu.be/aAzRGZqo8AY" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><em>5 Fine Frokner</em></a> was part of my obsession with the Norwegian TV show SKAM.)</p>

<p>Today I thought I'd share with you some of the songs from the <em>Everything We Need</em> soundtrack, just as I've done for the other books in the <em>Finding Forever</em> series (<a href="http://rebeccaraine.com/soundtracks/our-little-secret-soundtrack/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Our Little Secret</a>, <a href="http://rebeccaraine.com/soundtracks/lost-amber-soundtrack/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lost in Amber</a>, <a href="http://rebeccaraine.com/soundtracks/time-forever-soundtrack/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">This Time Forever</a>). Only this time I thought I'd add links to the songs through YouTube for your easy listening pleasure.</p>

<p>Without further ado, here is a list of about half the songs that helped me bring life to Trina, Brady and Jeremy while writing <em>Everything We Need</em>:</p>

<ul><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/xVKGXgHDMvQ" target="_blank">Mad About You - Hooverphonic</a> (This was the <em>Everything We Need</em> theme song. I've loved this quirky song for years and this book seemed like the perfect place to use it. Check out the film clip for a bizarre but fun story about a woman who falls in love with... a giant plant?)</li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/HoOOFgz8yO4" target="_blank">Slow Devour - Emptyself</a></li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/sahiA1hIokw" target="_blank">Oceandust - Hands Like Houses</a> (This was Jeremy's song every step of the way.)</li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/jir6yN6sLMU" target="_blank">Wicked Game - David Cook</a> (Brady's song for Trina. I love David Cook's deep, crooning voice.)</li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/X1Fqn9du7xo" target="_blank">Whataya Want from Me - Adam Lambert</a></li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/GL557mwEqqs" target="_blank">All You Wanted - Sounds Under Radio</a></li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/_yX2ov-hpnk" target="_blank">Breathe Me - Sia</a> (This was Trina's song. She was so in need of TLC from a good man - or two ;)</li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/v96wkt38EU8" target="_blank">Way Down We Go - Kaleo</a></li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/zP50Ewh31E4" target="_blank">Too Close - Alex Clare</a> (Another one for Jeremy)</li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/05vSgyzELMM" target="_blank">Bloodstream - Stateless</a></li><li><a rel="noopener" href="https://youtu.be/n12pydZoiAg" target="_blank">Bad Habit - The Kooks</a> (I got a really clear picture of Trina as she was at the start of the book every time I listened to this song.)</li></ul>
