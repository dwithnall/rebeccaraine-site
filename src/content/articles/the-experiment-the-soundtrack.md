<p>Music has always played a big part in my life and every book I write comes with its own soundtrack. Book soundtracks are invaluable as they keep me focused on the current book, even when I'm not writing. I listen to them when I'm driving in the car, cooking bacon and eggs for a big Sunday breakfast, even when I'm doing the ironing. Often little snippets of lyric will spark an idea that will turn out to be just the thing I need to solve a plot problem or figure out why a character is insisting on doing whatever it is they're doing (rather than what I want them to do). Today, I wanted to share the music that inspired me while I was writing <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08FB1JL3R" target="_blank">The Experiment</a>.</p>

<p>The whole soundtrack is available through my <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://open.spotify.com/user/rrainewriter?si=Giex4YnEQaqTdd6mGyTyKg" target="_blank">Spotify Profile</a>, or you can go straight to the playlist itself <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://open.spotify.com/playlist/7MMcd5QmG3z7Ba0B1vxQW2?si=cjRuEsPVS16OVMj4C0piIQ" target="_blank">here</a>.</p>
