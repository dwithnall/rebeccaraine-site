**Amber** loves her freedom too much to trade it for commitment — until Lincoln offers her something better: all the intensity of a real relationship, with a built-in expiry date.

**Lincoln** craves a depth of connection his own childhood never gave him, and he's willing to bend his rules to have it, even knowing it means loving Amber on borrowed time.
