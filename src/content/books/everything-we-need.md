**Trina** has decided logic, not instinct, will finally land her a happily-ever-after — which makes her inconvenient attraction to her new gay flatmate a serious problem.

**Jeremy** just needs a friend while he rebuilds his life, except he isn't nearly as gay as Trina thinks, and keeping his hands to himself is getting harder by the day.

**Brady** wants only to be left alone, until the neighbour next door turns out to be everything he's spent his life trying to escape — and everything he never knew he needed.
