**Julia** is a practical accountant with no patience for the salacious thoughts her two devastatingly hot bosses inspire — thoughts she fully intends to leave behind with her old job.

**Derek and Scott** have always known they were meant to share their lives with a woman, and they're certain Julia is her. Now that she's leaving their employ, nothing is stopping them from making their move.
