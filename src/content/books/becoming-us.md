**Lawrence** fell for Gabi and Connor at the same time when he was eighteen, and one reckless kiss cost him both of them. Six years later, he's still the same selfish bastard who wants more than his fair share of love — and he's not giving up his second chance.

**Gabi** rebuilt her friendship with Connor after Lawrence's betrayal, but a wedding weekend together has reawakened the attraction she's spent years denying.

**Connor** has been voted World's Sexiest Man twice, and Gabi has still only ever seen him as a friend — until a wedding date gives him one chance to change her mind, with Lawrence watching his every move.
