**Jeremy** has spent years trying and failing to save a man he loved, and he's finished putting anyone's needs above his own — which means staying far away from anyone as damaged as Harrison.

**Harrison** has built his sanity out of discipline and hard rules, but Jeremy's smile threatens to undo every one of them. Revealing the truth about his past could break the future he's just started to want.
