**Patrick** has always known exactly what he wants, and it's never been men — until losing a bet means kissing his best friend Logan, and one taste rewrites everything he thought he knew. As a trained psychologist, he knows exactly how to find the truth: further research.

**Logan** has spent years keeping Patrick safely in the friend zone, and one kiss shouldn't change that — except Patrick's proposed experiment into his own bisexuality needs a willing volunteer, and Logan isn't about to let anyone else conduct it.
