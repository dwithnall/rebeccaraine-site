**Johnny** did everything his parents wanted — respectable job, childhood sweetheart, rock star dreams shelved. Until his divorce cracked the cage open, and Calum walked in.

**Calum** knows getting involved with a client is forbidden. But when Johnny's band signs with his company, distance turns out to be the one thing neither of them can manage.
