**Kelly** watched Jake walk out on her a decade ago and swore she was over it — until his sudden return has her plotting the ultimate seduction, purely to reclaim her feminine power.

**Jake** wants a second chance at love, not just her body, so when Kelly draws a hard line at the bedroom door, he sets out to prove he's after her heart instead.
